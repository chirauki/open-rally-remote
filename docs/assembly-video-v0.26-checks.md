## Findings

- **Housing onto the stack (step 8), and the cover off (step 9).** The lower-facet cover-screw bosses (Ø6, axis along the facet normal, 20° from X) pass through R3.3 notches in the joystick board: 0.3 mm clearance. Moving the housing straight along X, the bosses hit the board from about 10 mm before the housing seats (19.4 mm³ at 6.6 mm). Straight paths from -5° to 25° from X, in 5° steps (the facet normal is at 20°), all collided (largest overlap 6.4-22.6 mm³ in the last 12 mm). Fitting the joystick group to the housing first, along its axis, and then closing the housing and the group onto the cover along X does not clear either: the stick pin then crosses the cover hole sideways (2.7 mm³ at 6 mm) and drags the sheet. The V0.26 check "lifted out with the cover" did not include the joystick board. Not solved; see "Open unknowns" in docs/mechanical-concept.md.
- **Vent label.** The Ø10 mm label envelope at X = -41 runs 3.2 mm into the lower-facet stack at the bottom wall: cover 1.2 mm³, joystick plate 2.0 mm³, keypad 1.3 mm³.
- **Joystick board J1 against the housing.** 0.015 mm³ (about 0.1 mm deep) at X = -35.5, Y = -31.2. Within the accuracy of the socket envelope (2.9 mm tall, assumed).
- **USB-C board path (step 2).** Slid straight along its rib from the cavity opening, the board's edges hit the upper cover-screw bosses at Y = 24 (19 mm³). Carried 1.5 mm above the rib past the bosses, lowered onto the rib 8 mm short of the rear wall, and slid in, it clears. The margin is small: 1 mm above the rib still overlaps 0.2 mm³. The video uses this path.
